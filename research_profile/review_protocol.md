# Initial Review Protocol

## Stage 1: map the field

Collect recent high-quality reviews and a small set of foundational papers. Build a taxonomy of material architectures, failure modes, and practical validation levels.

## Stage 2: targeted evidence collection

Search each evidence track separately. Screen title and abstract first, deduplicate by DOI and normalized title, then score candidates for relevance, evidence quality, and practical applicability.

## Stage 3: structured deep reading

For every promoted paper, capture the research question, material recipe, electrode parameters, cell configuration, controls, quantitative results, degradation evidence, limitations, and source locations. Separate reported facts from reviewer inference.

## Stage 4: cross-paper synthesis

Create comparison tables only after normalizing units and test conditions. Do not compare capacity retention without noting cycle count, rate, temperature, voltage window, loading, cell format, and retention definition.

## Stage 5: gap analysis

Identify claims that lack practical full-cell support, conflicts between studies, missing scale-up evidence, and the experiments or disclosures needed to resolve uncertainty.

## Initial deliverables

- Field taxonomy and terminology map.
- Evidence table of representative material systems.
- Practicality matrix from material-level proof to cell-level validation.
- Degradation-mechanism map.
- Manufacturing and commercialization constraints.
- Prioritized open questions and recommended next searches.
