# Research Scope

## Objective

Build an evidence-grounded view of silicon-carbon negative electrodes that can meet traction-battery requirements for energy density, fast charging, cycle life, safety, manufacturability, and cost.

## Core questions

1. Which silicon-carbon architectures best control volume change while retaining practical reversible capacity?
2. How do silicon content, particle morphology, porosity, conductive network, binder, electrolyte, and prelithiation interact?
3. Which degradation pathways dominate under high loading, low excess electrolyte, fast charge, and wide-temperature cycling?
4. Which results remain credible when moving from half cells to practical full cells and larger formats?
5. What manufacturing, supply-chain, safety, and cost constraints limit commercial adoption?

## Evidence tracks

- Material architecture: silicon nanoparticles, silicon oxide, porous silicon, carbon coatings, graphite-silicon blends, and composite granules.
- Interface control: artificial interphases, electrolyte additives, localized high-concentration electrolytes, and formation protocols.
- Electrode engineering: binders, conductive additives, calendering, porosity, areal loading, thickness, and current collectors.
- Lithium inventory: chemical, electrochemical, and electrode-integrated prelithiation.
- Degradation and safety: particle fracture, electrical isolation, interphase growth, gas generation, impedance rise, thermal stability, and swelling.
- Practical validation: full-cell pairing, negative-to-positive capacity ratio, electrolyte amount, formation, fast charge, temperature window, and pack-relevant lifetime metrics.
- Scale-up: material yield, process compatibility, quality control, intellectual-property landscape, cost, and environmental burden.

## Inclusion criteria

- Peer-reviewed papers, authoritative reviews, standards, patents, and credible industrial technical reports.
- Quantitative electrochemical or manufacturing evidence relevant to silicon-carbon negative electrodes.
- Studies that disclose enough test conditions to interpret performance.
- Priority to 2018-present, while retaining foundational earlier work.

## Exclusion criteria

- Silicon used only in positive-electrode, separator, sensor, or non-battery applications.
- Results without identifiable cell chemistry or test conditions.
- Performance claims supported only by idealized low-loading half cells, unless used to explain a mechanism.
- Duplicate preprints when a final peer-reviewed version is available.

## Practical-data checklist

Record silicon fraction, active-material loading, electrode density, areal capacity, initial efficiency, counter electrode, positive-electrode chemistry, capacity ratio, electrolyte amount, formation protocol, voltage window, temperature, charge/discharge rate, retention criterion, swelling, and safety observations whenever reported.
