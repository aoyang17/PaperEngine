Model and Physical Mechanisms模型与物理机制

Model framework模型框架

A non-conserved Ginzburg-Landau phase field with orientation-dependent gradient energy is coupled bidirectionally to a heat-diffusion equation through temperature-dependent driving and latent-heat release.模型将方向相关梯度能的非守恒 Ginzburg–Landau 相场与热扩散方程双向耦合：温度控制相变驱动力，相变速率释放潜热。

Evidence: Model, p.2 (equation); Model, p.3 (equation); Model, p.4 (equation); Model, p.3 (body text) | confidence: high

Model assumptions模型假设

  • The material is a pure melt and one thermal diffusion field limits growth; convection, Marangoni flow, and transformation expansion or shrinkage are neglected.材料为纯熔体，唯一限制生长的体场是热扩散；忽略对流、Marangoni 流及相变膨胀或收缩。 Evidence: Abstract and Introduction, p.1 (body text) | confidence: high
  • Thermal diffusivity is identical in solid and liquid, and the bulk phase minima remain fixed at p=0 and p=1 so latent heat is released only where the interface passes.固液热扩散率相同，体相稳定值固定为 p=0 和 p=1，使潜热只在界面经过时释放。 Evidence: Model, p.2 (body text); Model, p.4 (equation) | confidence: high
  • The diffuse interface is thin relative to resolved morphology, while stochastic forcing is localized by p(1-p) and therefore acts mainly near the interface.弥散界面应远薄于待解析形貌，随机项乘以 p(1−p)，因此主要作用于界面附近。 Evidence: Simulations, p.4 (body text); Discussions, p.13 (body text) | confidence: high

Free energy and thermodynamics自由能与热力学框架

  • The Ginzburg-Landau functional combines a gradient penalty with a quartic double-well potential whose stable minima are liquid p=0 and solid p=1.Ginzburg–Landau 泛函由梯度惩罚和四次双阱势组成，双阱在液相 p=0 与固相 p=1 处稳定。 Evidence: Model, p.2 (equation); Model, p.2 (body text) | confidence: high
  • The potential-depth difference is proportional to m, so m supplies thermodynamic phase preference; orientation dependence in epsilon converts interfacial energy into anisotropic capillarity.势阱深度差与 m 成正比，m 提供相的热力学偏好；ε 的方向依赖把界面能转化为各向异性毛细作用。 Evidence: Model, p.2 (equation); Model, p.3 (body text) | confidence: high

Results, Validation, and Mechanisms结果、验证与机理

Key results关键结果

  • Increasing four-fold anisotropy from zero to 0.050 reorganizes growth from isotropic finger splitting to oriented dendrites with increasingly strong branch screening and preferred directions.四重各向异性从 0 增至 0.050 时，形貌从各向同性指进逐渐转为强定向枝晶，分枝屏蔽和择优方向明显增强。 Evidence: Dendrite growth, p.8 (figure) | confidence: high
  • Increasing K destabilizes planar or compact fronts and produces cells, slits, tip splitting, and progressively thinner branching patterns in the sampled thermal regimes.K 增大使平面或紧致前沿失稳，在研究区间内依次出现胞状、狭缝、尖端分裂和更细的分枝形貌。 Evidence: Simulations, p.4 (body text); Dendrite growth, p.8 (figure) | confidence: high
  • Noise amplitude strongly changes side-branch selection for non-oscillating tips, whereas oscillatory tip growth makes branching less noise-sensitive.在非振荡尖端条件下，噪声幅值显著改变侧枝选择；尖端振荡存在时，分枝对噪声较不敏感。 Evidence: Discussions, p.12 (body text); Discussions, p.12 (figure) | confidence: high

Validation and comparison验证与对比

  • Validation is primarily qualitative: simulated shapes resemble familiar dendrites and reproduce expected smoothing, instability, preferred-direction, screening, and side-branch phenomena.验证主要是定性的：模拟形貌类似常见枝晶，并再现平滑、失稳、择优方向、屏蔽和侧枝等预期现象。 Evidence: Dendrite growth, p.8 (figure); Discussions, p.12 (body text) | confidence: high
  • The asymptotic sharp-interface law provides theoretical consistency between the diffuse equation and thermodynamic driving plus anisotropic curvature, but no grid-converged velocity comparison is reported.尖锐界面渐近式为弥散方程与热力学驱动力/各向异性曲率之间提供理论一致性，但没有网格收敛的速度对比。 Evidence: Model, p.3 (equation); Discussions, p.13 (body text) | confidence: high
  • No direct fit to a named material experiment, measured tip velocity, branch spacing, or thermal history is provided.论文没有拟合具体材料实验，也没有比较实测尖端速度、分枝间距或温度历程。 Evidence: Discussions, p.12 (body text); Discussions, p.13 (body text) | confidence: high

Mechanistic interpretation机理解释

  • Anisotropic capillary derivatives magnify even small angular modulation, selecting branch orientation and permitting corners when d0(theta) becomes negative.各向异性毛细函数中的角向导数会放大很小的调制，从而选择分枝方向；当 d₀(θ) 变负时可形成尖角。 Evidence: Discussions, p.12 (figure); Discussions, p.12 (body text) | confidence: high
  • Latent heat reduces local supercooling and couples neighboring tips through the temperature field, creating competition and screening for access to cooling.潜热降低局部过冷度，并通过温度场耦合相邻尖端，使其围绕散热能力发生竞争和屏蔽。 Evidence: Model, p.4 (equation); Discussions, p.12 (body text) | confidence: high
  • Noise does not set the main-tip velocity in the non-oscillating case but biases which young side branches survive competition; a deterministic tip oscillation can instead dominate branch production.非振荡情形下噪声不控制主尖端速度，却会偏置年轻侧枝的存活；确定性尖端振荡则可成为更强的分枝来源。 Evidence: Discussions, p.12 (body text); Discussions, p.12 (figure) | confidence: high
